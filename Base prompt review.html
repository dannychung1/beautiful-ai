Art direction · review

Base prompt

This is the professional prompt the builder sends to ChatGPT, taken apart line by line. The proposed version is now live in the builder, and rules 02 and 07 and check 8 on the Art direction page match it. The product and presentation prompts carry the same negative line.

Reference

  • What it gets right. Golden-hour sun, at sunrise or sunset, spotlights the primary professional, and the room falls off around them. The gaze is on a colleague, and the foreground glass and laptop are soft.
  • What the prompt has to prevent. The wall sign reads as near-legible words. Our images never contain writing or words, so the prompt ends with a negative line that bans all of it.
  • What we are adding. The frame proves the light can wash the room and the window can glow brighter than the face, so neither is a hard rule anymore.

Skin and light references

What crisp, real skin looks like

In these frames, directional light draws hard highlights on the forehead, cheekbones and nose, and the shadows stay open with detail. Pores and fine hair are visible, and light glows through the edges of the ears. The prompt names each of these, so the model renders them without a filter.

The 2 newest portraits add the close-up recipe. The subject is seated, shot from just below eye level through a soft foreground edge, with a genuine smile in hard side light and a bright, open space falling out of focus behind them.

Current vs proposed

The prompt, with default picks

Blue text marks the parts the builder fills in. The proposed version labels every line, because ChatGPT follows labelled instructions more closely than a paragraph.

Current

A documentary editorial photograph of a marketing lead in their 30s, explaining with open hands, in a warm office with plain plaster walls and wood.
Photographed from seated table height through a softly blurred foreground element.
Gaze on a colleague or task just out of frame.
They are in the light: face turned toward clear raking sunlight with crisp-edged shadows, with the brightest, warmest skin tones in the image on their face, so the light spotlights them in their element. The light may fall off into shadow or softly wash the space around them.
Windows and lamps in the frame may glow brighter.
Tailored professional clothing in quiet neutrals, with one small accent of vivid cerulean blue on earrings.
Any laptop secondary to the person, screen unreadable, matte deep navy, plain lid.
Background signage soft and illegible.
Natural skin texture, rich warm contrast, true-to-life color. Candid, unposed.
Shot on 50mm at f/2.8, waist-up, desk and room visible around them.

Proposed

A candid documentary photograph of a marketing lead in their 30s, building a presentation on a laptop, in a warm office with plain plaster walls and wood.
Story: an editorial magazine photograph about the new professional way to present. They are confident, prepared and at ease while preparing, sharing or talking through their work, never scrambling or stressed.
Cast: choose a real body size and build for this person, anywhere from slim to plus-size, and when it fits, a visible or assistive detail such as a wheelchair, cane, hearing aid or prosthetic, shown as a natural part of their work and never as the subject of the image. Heritage shows only through their real features, such as face, skin, hair and build, never through cultural or traditional jewelry, beadwork, patterns or dress. The setting and props stay the same for every heritage, with no cultural artwork, textiles, objects or decor used to signal it.
Frame: Shot on 50mm at f/2.8, solo and waist-up, building a presentation or presenting one. A laptop or screen can be in frame or only implied, and the desk and room stay visible around them.
Copy space: leave clean, low-detail space above the subject for a headline to be placed in post.
Camera: seated table height, looking past a softly blurred foreground object such as a glass, a mug, a plant or a colleague's shoulder.
Gaze: on a colleague or the task just out of frame, never at the lens.
Light: low, clear sun rakes in from the side and spotlights them in their element. It is either early-morning light that feels full of possibility or late-afternoon light that feels relaxed and confident, whichever suits the moment. The sun itself is never in frame: no sun disc, sunburst, lens flare or sunrise or sunset sky. Only its light shows, falling across the scene. Their face is turned toward it and is the brightest, warmest skin in the frame. It draws long, crisp-edged shadows and picks out the texture of walls, wood, fabric and skin. Around them the light falls off into deep, rich shadow, and windows may glow brighter than the face.
Wardrobe: current, tailored workwear of your choosing, with a fabric and cut that suit this person and setting, such as fine-gauge knitwear, a relaxed wool blazer, crisp cotton poplin, washed silk or soft suede, in quiet neutrals. It reads as 2020s professional, never formal-event or costume.
Accent: 1 small accent color taking no more than 10% of the frame, in a shade or tint of cerulean blue (#00B9FF) or fuchsia-magenta rose (#DB2475). Choose which one, how deep or soft it is and where it sits, on clothing, an accessory or an object in the room, so it fits the mood, light and atmosphere of the scene.
Devices: a laptop can sit in frame as part of the work. It stays secondary to the person, a standard silver, grey or black with a plain lid, and its screen is never readable, because the product is not the focus of this image.
Background: no alcohol.
Never include: writing, words, letters, numbers, logos or signage of any kind, anywhere in the frame.
Skin and detail: shot with a fast prime lens, with Kodak Portra 400 skin tones. The raking light draws crisp highlights on the forehead, cheekbones, nose and lips, rich midtones across the face and shadows on the face that keep their detail, while the room around them falls into deep shadow. The face is in sharp focus. Visible pores, fine facial hair, small lines, freckles and natural variation in skin tone hold up at 100%, and light glows through the edges of the ears and hair. The deep shadow belongs to the room, never the face. No retouching, skin smoothing, soft glow or HDR look.
Look: shot on medium-format film, with the deep, saturated shadows and amber warmth of Kodachrome 64 and the true, natural skin tones of Kodak Portra 400. Color stays true to life, and the grain is fine and even. The frame feels like warm sun on skin.
Finish: unposed, caught mid-moment, never posed for the camera.
