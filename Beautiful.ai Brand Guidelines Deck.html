04 / Logo · Construction

Clearspace & minimum size.

Clearspace — 1×
Reserve clearspace equal to one x — the height of the period — on every side. Nothing else lives in this margin.
x
x
Minimum size
The wordmark holds to 24px minimum. Below that, switch to the symbol — it keeps the brand intact at favicon scale.

05 / Color · The pair
You
AI

Cyan is you. Magenta is the AI.

A signature pair across marketing, brand, and product. Cyan is the professional doing the work — the user, their input, their voice. Magenta is the AI that meets them halfway. Together, they signal the partnership: a human and the model, making something better than either could alone.

07 / Iconography

Clean, geometric line icons.

Outlined, rounded joins, even 1.6px stroke on a 24px grid. No emoji, no decorative illustration. Inherit text color; cyan only when calling for action.

Sparkle · layers · present · chart · image · edit · share · secure — a representative set. The full library lives in the product and in Figma.
