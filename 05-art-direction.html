Art direction

We show professionals in their element, lit well and doing real work, alongside our product and the slides they build in it. We use photography because professionals' work is real. We support it with icons and lightweight spot illustrations drawn from our product. We never use cartoons or abstract illustration, because they make professionals' work look imagined.

Art direction

Imagery

In their best light

Our approach is documentary storytelling made ready for commercial use.

Professionals at work

We tell professionals' stories the way a documentary photographer would, caught active, mid-task, and never posed. Setting and mood serve the story as much as light does. Early morning light hints at the promise of the day, and sun-drenched afternoons give us the ease of work well-handled.

When we show a team, one professional leads the frame. They are in sharp focus and catch the best light, and their colleagues listen in softer light around them. The story is their idea moving the team forward.

Product in the light

When focusing on our product, we show the real interface in our brand light. The feature professionals use is in sharp focus, and a soft glow of Agency Azul and Ready Rose surrounds it on Base Blue.

Every interface is a real screenshot from our current platform. We never mock up a feature, and we never show a control that professionals can't use today.

Presentations in abstract

When focusing on our slides, we place them in a big, open space, captured in concept of nimbleness and endlessness. Thin slides floating, flying or pulling apart in depth and running past the edge of the frame.

We use open space when the finished work is the hero, with nothing in the frame but our slides and our brand light. The background is Gallery Grey or Base Blue, and a soft glow of Agency Azul and Ready Rose fills no more than 20% of the frame.

How we look

These hold for every image and every shot type. Break one and the image doesn't ship.

01
Light

Turn the professional’s face toward the light so it spotlights them. Let the light fall off or wash the space around them as the setting needs. The warm sun sets our pros apart from the cool neutrals of our product and slides.

02
Camera

Shoot from seated table height past a soft foreground object, such as a glass, a mug or a colleague’s shoulder, so we feel in the room.

03
Keep it candid

We photograph pros mid-action or mid-gesture, the way a documentary photographer would, so every image shows real work in progress. Nobody poses or sits idle for our camera.

04
Composition

Hold 1 hero with room to breathe, and place their eye line on a third. When we show 2 or more colleagues, the light favors the professional the story is about.

05
Casting and wardrobe

Cast wide across role, age, geography, ability and body size, so every professional sees their team in our frames. Dress them in classic, timeless workwear in quiet neutrals, with an accent of Azul or Rose that fills no more than 10% of the frame. We show heritage only through real features, never through cultural jewelry, dress, decor or props.

06
Devices and screens

A laptop or phone sits in frame as a secondary part of the work, with its screen soft or turned away. When the story is our product or a presentation, the screen becomes the hero, in sharp focus with a real screenshot placed in post. Words and graphics appear only on that screen, and the rest of the frame has no writing, signage, logos or numbers.

01
Real screenshots

We show only real screenshots from our current platform. We never mock up a feature, and we never show a control that professionals can’t use today.

02
1 feature in focus

Show 1 feature at full size and in sharp focus, so professionals see exactly what it does. The slide or workspace behind it sits soft.

03
Our brand light

Set the product on Gallery Grey or Base Blue, with a soft glow of Agency Azul and Ready Rose around the feature in focus.

01
Real slides

Every slide we show is one professionals can build in our platform today. We generate blank slide faces and place real slides on them in post.

02
Endless and nimble

Show thin slides floating, flying or pulling apart in depth, and let them run past the edge of the frame so the deck feels endless.

03
Our brand light

Set slides on Gallery Grey or Base Blue. A soft glow of Agency Azul and Ready Rose fills no more than 20% of the frame.

Real product, real slides
Place our product and slides on blank screens. To place them with AI, attach the generated image first and the real one second, then run the matching prompt.
Place a product screenshot
Edit the first attached image. Place the second attached image, our real product screenshot, onto the blank white laptop screen. Keep everything else in the first image exactly as it is, including the people, framing, light, color and grain.
Fit: pin the screenshot to the 4 corners of the screen and match its exact perspective and angle. Never stretch, crop or redraw the interface.
Light: match the scene's color temperature and exposure. Carry the same light falloff across the screen, and let any shadow or raking light that crosses the screen fall across it too. Keep the display slightly brighter than its surroundings, as a lit screen is.
Reflections: add a faint, soft reflection of the room and the light source on the glass, never strong enough to hide the interface.
Noise: add the same film grain and noise as the rest of the photo, so the screen never looks pasted on.
Text: keep every word in the screenshot sharp and legible. Never restyle, translate or invent any part of the interface.
Place real slides
Edit the first attached image. Place the other attached images, our real slides, onto the blank white slide faces, 1 per slide, in the order attached. Keep everything else in the first image exactly as it is, including the background, spotlights, framing and grain.
Fit: pin each slide to the 4 corners of its slide face and match that face's exact perspective, angle and depth. Never stretch, crop or redraw a slide.
Light: match each slide to the light where it sits. Let the blue and magenta spotlights fall softly across the slides they touch, and keep the contact shadows and edge highlights already in the scene.
Reflections: slides are matte, so add only a faint sheen where a spotlight hits a face directly.
Noise: add the same grain and noise as the rest of the image, so the slides never look pasted on.
Text: keep every word on the slides sharp and legible. Never restyle, translate or invent any content.

Art direction
Motion

  

    
We use motion to show professionals what changed and where to look next. Every movement is subtle and functional, so it never slows down the work of building a deck.

    
How we move
We hold every animation to 6 principles, and the best work meets all of them at once.

    
Emotion
We move to give professionals the feelings they want from their work, such as the excitement of a new idea or the satisfaction of a finished deck.
Competence
We move with intention, showing our mastery and command to earn trust.
Clarity
We move to feature, focus, and to deepen understanding.
Ease
We move with an air of effortlessness and comfort, so building a presentation never feels like a struggle with the tool.
Nimbleness
We move like the smart and flexible platform we are, adapting each layout as teams add and rearrange their content.
Purpose
We move only with intent. Every motion serves the story, an interaction or feedback, and we cut anything that does not.

    
Decks in motion
Our decks always look endless and nimble, in stills and in motion.

    
We show a professional's deck as thin, light slides that run past the frame or recede into depth. The slides can float, fly, twist or pull apart in an exploded view, so professionals see how much they can build and how fast it comes together. We never show a deck as a heavy or static pile. In a still, freeze the slides mid-motion. In motion, let them travel through the frame and resolve into the headline or the logo.
