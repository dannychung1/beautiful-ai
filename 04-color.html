You × AI

The signature pair

Cyan is you. Magenta is the AI.

A signature pair we use across marketing, brand, and product. Cyan stands for the professional doing the work — the user, their input, their voice. Magenta stands for the AI that meets them halfway — generative, helpful, tireless. When the two appear together, the message is the partnership: a human and the model, making something better than either could alone.

Brand gradient

The cyan-to-magenta glow.

A soft radial blend of the signature pair, fading to white or teal. Reserved for hero moments and generative/AI surfaces — never as a background for dense text.
