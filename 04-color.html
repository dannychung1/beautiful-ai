Color

Our colors serve to provide a platform to show off the brilliance of professionals' slide stories and our product features.

Palette

We lead with our 5 brand colors and support them with 1 neutral ramp, so you know which color to reach for first. Use Deepest Blue wherever a design calls for black, and for text.

Professional x AI

A color story for how we work

We pair these two colors across marketing, brand, and product. Azul represents the professional leading the work with their input and their story while Rose stands for the AI that helps them build it. We show the two together when the point is that a professional and the AI made the work jointly.

Primary palette usage

We use our primary colors as the ground on every surface we publish.

Accent palette usage

We show accents where pros act on a slide and where our AI works with them.

Neutral palette usage

Our neutrals provide clarity and structure without sacrificing the brand.
