Typography

We set the brand in Post Grotesk, designed for maximum usability and an overall sense of neutrality. Our words stay out of the way so professionals' slide stories can shine.

Post Grotesk

Josh Finklea drew Post Grotesk as a contemporary grotesk, with flared terminals taken from Bauer's Venus. We set every heading and every line of body copy in it.

Post Grotesk Book, label style

We set labels, metadata, and chapter numbers in Post Grotesk Book at label sizes, uppercase, tracked open to 0.12em. This is the one typeface doing a second job, so pros read one voice across the whole guide.

Display tier

Set display type at 95% leading and −0.035em tracking.

Nothing goes tighter than 95%.

We use display for covers, chapter openers, and statement pages. Below 95% leading a descender lands on the line beneath it.

Size120px and up
Leading95%
Tracking−0.035em

Optical alignment

Left-align display type to the ink, not the text box. Pull each line left by its own glyph side bearing — roughly 0.08em for B, 0.05em for G — so the stems land on the grid.

Letter spacing

Tight at display. Open at small.

Display type runs at -0.03em to feel deliberate. Body stays at 0em. Eyebrows and labels open up to 0.08–0.10em and uppercase, to feel like signage.
