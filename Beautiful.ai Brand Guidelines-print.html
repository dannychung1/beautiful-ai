Foreword

A presentation is the professional's tool for impact.

This is the operating manual for the Beautiful.ai brand. It defines how we look, sound, and behave across every surface — from a venture pitch to a keynote, from the editor to the boardroom. Use it to keep our work unmistakably ours.

01 / Compass

What we're here to do.

Three statements anchor every decision we make about this brand — the mission we're chasing, the vision we're building, and the values we work by.

Mission
Why we exist — the change we're trying to make in the world.
To change the way the world presents.

Presentations are how decisions get made — and how the wrong ones get made when the storytelling lets the substance down. We're here to change that, one finished deck at a time.

Vision
Where we're headed — the future we're building toward.
A world where every professional, regardless of resources, can build presentations that work.

No design team? No design degree? No problem. The product, the templates, the AI, and this brand all exist to make a great deck a question of having something to say — not of having a studio behind you.

Values
How we work — the four things every Beautiful.ai surface should embody.

Decks are built in groups. We design for the second pair of eyes, the third comment, the fourth revision.

02
Design

A bias toward beautiful. If the work has to choose between cleverness and clarity, clarity wins every time.

03

Kerning, alignment, the period after the headline. The small things are what people feel before they read.

Composure is the brand. Confident, considered, never trying too hard — in copy, in product, in the room.

03 / Voice & Tone

How we sound when we write.

Three principles guide every sentence — from a tooltip to a homepage hero. They're not rules; they're a temperament.

Principle 01
Direct, never dry.

Sentences earn their place. We get to the point first and decorate second. If a word isn't doing work, we cut it.

✓Build a deck in minutes. Not a weekend.
✕Unlock unparalleled productivity with our cutting-edge platform.
Principle 02
Confident, never corporate.

We speak to peers, not to procurement. We use second person and everyday language. The product is sophisticated; the writing isn't.

✓You bring the story. We'll handle the slide.
✕Our enterprise-grade solution empowers stakeholders.
Principle 03
Optimistic, never breathless.

We believe presentations can be better. We say so plainly. No exclamation marks, no hype emoji, no shouting in caps.

✓It's faster than starting from scratch. And it looks better, too.
✕🚀 GAME-CHANGING AI THAT WILL BLOW YOUR MIND!!!
Sentence case

Headlines, labels, buttons. Never ALL CAPS for body copy.

Numerals

"3 ways to…" not "three ways to…". Numbers get crisper.

No emoji

The brand is emoji-free. Iconography does the lifting instead.

Active verbs

CTAs lead with the verb. Get started. See pricing. Talk to sales.

Six ways to break the signature.

The wordmark is a system, not a toy. These mistakes are common — and they undo years of equity in a single export.

A two-color story

Cyan is you. Magenta is the AI.

A signature pair we use across marketing, brand, and product. Cyan stands for the professional doing the work — the user, their input, their voice. Magenta stands for the AI that meets them halfway — generative, helpful, tireless. When the two appear together, the message is the partnership: a human and the model, making something better than either could alone.

Letter spacing

Tight at display.
Open at small.

Display type runs at -0.03em to feel deliberate. Body stays at 0em. Eyebrows and labels open up to 0.08–0.10em and uppercase, to feel like signage.

07 / Iconography

Material Symbols, rounded.

We use the Material Symbols icon library, outlined and lightly rounded. No emoji, no decorative illustration, no hand-drawn glyphs. Consistency over cleverness.

08 / Imagery

The brand archetype.

Before words, before product, there is a character that walks into the room. Imagery is how we render that character — not in pictures of people, but in the marks, motifs, and surfaces we carry from slide to surface to story.

Archetype · One sentence
We are The Finisher.

Beautiful.ai exists for professionals who close and finish strong. The character that shows up before we say a word is one of composure under pressure — the steady hand that takes a draft across the line into a piece of work someone is proud to send.

ComposedNever frantic. Always considered.
DecisiveEditing out, not piling on.

Expressions

Three ways the archetype shows up.

These aren't photographs — they're the visual vocabulary the archetype carries from one surface to the next. Use them as the building blocks for imagery before reaching for any image at all.

Motif 01 — The dot

The moment of finish.

The cyan period from the wordmark, multiplied. A pattern of small acts of finishing — used as decoration only when it earns a meaning.

Motif 02 — The mark

Quiet at every scale.

The mark stays itself from favicon to billboard. The same proportions, the same dot. Confidence that doesn't need volume.

Motif 03 — Resolution

From rough to ready.

A visual idiom we own: a progression from raw to resolved, ending in the brand's cyan. Every story we tell ends in finished work.

The archetype, personified

What "finishing strong" looks like in the wild.

Reference figures from outside our category who embody the archetype's composure under pressure. We don't try to look like them — we try to make work that feels like them: prepared, intentional, delivered.

Composition

Single subject, room to breathe. Eye line at the rule of thirds, never centered like a passport photo.

Lighting

Natural where we can. Editorial cool wash for brand layer, neutral key for product, soft daylight for content.

Crop

16:10 cinematic for brand. 4:3 for product. 3:4 portrait for content. One crop per layer, never mixed.

Diversity

Wide in role, age, geography. We design for everyone whose week ends in a deck.

Before you ship

A short pre-flight checklist.

01
Which layer am I in?Brand, product, or content? Voice and density follow.
02
Does the wordmark have room?One x of clearspace on every side. No exceptions.
03
Is cyan doing one job?Highlight one action per surface. Save it for what matters.
04
Would our audience see themselves?If the imagery and copy don't reflect them, rewrite.
