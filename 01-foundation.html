Foundation

Beautiful.ai is for professionals who need a new way to work.

We're on a mission to change the way the world presents.

We see a world where every professional has a thinking partner.

Who we're for

We build every decision around the professionals, teams, and leaders who present for a living and work outside design. They arrive with a topic, an outline drafted elsewhere, or a document ready to become a deck, and we meet them at whichever door they walk in.

Voice & tone

Three traits define how we sound on every channel and how we refer to professionals and their work.

Trait 01
Plainspoken.

We speak to professionals at their own level: busy, smart, mid-task. We use their vocabulary, skip the preamble, and start from what they already know.

✓You already know the pitch. Here's the deck, ready.
✕Great news! Let's dive into this awesome new feature!
Trait 02
Declarative.

We think hard and then we commit. We say the direct thing once and let it stand, and every sentence states something a professional could disagree with.

✓Build a deck in minutes. Not a weekend.
✕Unlock unparalleled productivity with our cutting-edge platform.
Trait 03
Steady.

We stay the same voice while everything around our customers changes. We back the professional's judgment and credit the expertise they own, and our confidence comes from the work being handled.

✓Upload failed. Your work is saved. Try again.
✕Oops! Something went wrong 😬
spellcheck
Words we use

We use the same words for professionals and their work on every surface.

Professionals, pros, teams, leaders. We call our customers what they are, and we use whichever of the 4 the sentence needs. We never write users, folks, or people who.

Slides, smart slide layouts, presentations, decks. We call the work what it is on the screen in front of them, and we pick the word that fits the context.

Full sentences by default. We write complete sentences so that a clipped phrase still lands when we use one. We vary the rhythm and we never stack 3 fragments in a row.
