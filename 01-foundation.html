Brand Guidelines · Updated 5/26

Foundation

Our brand core starts with the professionals we serve and the presentations they need to win. Every choice we make serves these guiding principles.

Beautiful.ai is for professionals who need a new way to work.

MissionWe're on a mission to change the way the world presents.

VisionWe see a world where every professional has a thinking partner.

Who we're for

We build every decision around the professionals, teams and leaders who present for a living. They arrive with an idea or an outline that needs a deck. We make building one easy, whichever door they walk in.

On brand

Setting the new standard for presentations at work takes a committed point of view and promise. We get professionals to work ready. With an AI thinking partner and designer expertise built in, our platform provides speed and structure from the start. The result is presentations that are on time, on brand, and ready to impress.

Principles

grid_viewDesign

Good design is infrastructure. Every artifact we make is simple, scalable, and effective, and the distance between good enough and beautiful is the promise we keep.

routeStory

Every slide is a piece of a narrative. Layouts, data, and visuals earn their place by moving the story forward, and decks always read as one argument.

handshakePartnership

AI drafts. People decide. Our workflows draft, offering real, flexible directions, and judgment stays in human hands as the work moves between people.

boltSpeed

Fast and good arrive together. We expect both of ourselves and our platform, because modern work demands both at once.

Our archetype

The Working Sage

We know how good work gets built, and we put that knowledge in your hands. We start from your story, your data, and your brand. We hand you the right tool for the job: smart slides that lay themselves out, AI throughout, a template for every format. And we hold one standard of excellence across the team, whether the deck is a pitch, an intro, or a marketing push. You bring the story. We get it work ready.

Voice & tone

Three traits define how we sound on every channel and how we refer to professionals and their work.

Trait 01
Plainspoken.

We say it straight and cut every word that doesn't help professionals get their work done. We use their own vocabulary and start from what they already know.

checkYou already know the pitch. Here's the deck, ready.
closeGreat news! Let's dive into this awesome new feature!
Trait 02
Declarative.

We promise an outcome and state it with confidence. We say the direct thing once and let it stand, so professionals know exactly what they get.

checkBuild a deck in minutes, not a weekend.
closeUnlock unparalleled productivity with our cutting-edge platform.
Trait 03
Human.

We write to professionals with respect, the way a trusted colleague would. We understand the problem behind their work before we suggest solutions.

checkLet's build a better way.
closeLet me teach you a lesson.

Storytelling

Every brand story we tell comes from one or more facets: the presentation, the professional and the product.

PresentationHero when the story is about what

We show relevant content that reflects what our professionals are actually working on.

ProfessionalHero when the story is about who

We show professionals in the roles and scenarios they recognize as their own, so every frame reflects the work they actually do.

ProductHero when the story is about how

We show the features and ways of working that help professionals make great slides.
