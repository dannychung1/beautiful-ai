Brand guidelines · Layout

Layout grid

We build every page of the guide on 1 centered content column split into 12 columns. Use the same column, gutters and vertical spacing when you make assets for professionals so they read with the same rhythm as the guide.

The content column

Our site and the guide share 1 grid. At 1440px both put 84% of the screen in content, with 12 columns and 24px gutters. Marketing pages stop growing at 1200px, and the guide keeps growing to 2280px.

Label rail

When a block pairs a label with content, the label takes 3 of the 12 columns and the content takes 9.

3 columnsLabel, name or note
9 columnsSpecimen, image or content

Tile grids divide the 12 columns evenly: 2 tiles span 6 each, 3 span 4, 4 span 3 and 6 span 2. Corner radius follows the span, with 18px for 2-column elements, 12px for 3 and 4 columns and 8px for small cards.
