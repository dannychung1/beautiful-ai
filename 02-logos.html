Logos

Our logo is the wordmark in lowercase, anchored by a period — a quiet nod to the completion of a thought, and start of the next great one.

The symbol

A lowercase b with a cyan period — our entire belief system, condensed. The b is for beautiful. The dot is the moment a draft becomes finished work.

Clearspace

Keep 1× the height of the b.

Around the wordmark, reserve clearspace equal to one x — the height of the b — on every side. Nothing else lives in this margin.

Minimum size

Never below 80px wide.

The wordmark needs room for its letterforms — 80px wide is the floor. When space gets tighter than that, drop to the symbol; the b‑with‑dot keeps the brand intact at favicon scale. And the logo should never take up more than 10% of any composition.

Do

Four rules that keep it right.

Highest contrast

Always pick the logo version with the most contrast against its background.

1× clearspace

Keep clearspace equal to the height of the "b" on all four sides.

80px minimum

Never reproduce the wordmark narrower than 80px wide.

10% of the frame

The logo should occupy no more than a tenth of any composition.

Don't

Eight ways to break the signature.

The wordmark is a system. Don't distort or tilt it, don't remake or recolor it, don't add effects, don't combine it with other logos, don't set our name as text inside a sentence — write "Beautiful.ai" — and never place it on a low-contrast background.
